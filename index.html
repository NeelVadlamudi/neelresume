University of Maryland Baltimore County (opens in a new tab)

Master of Professional Studies in Data Science · Aug 2024–May 2026

Public research university in Maryland, between Baltimore and Washington. Strong in computing and applied graduate programs that sit next to real industry work.

The Master of Professional Studies in Data Science is a professional master's for people coming from different backgrounds. It walks you from raw data through analysis, modeling, and decisions you can defend at work.

Core covers introduction to data science, machine learning, big-data platforms, data management, ethics, leadership, and a capstone. Then three pathway courses in a focus area such as healthcare analytics, management science, or advanced computing.

An Institute of National Importance under India's Ministry of Education. A focused engineering campus in Delhi that runs B.Tech through postgraduate and doctoral programs.
